UC Irvine · APSA Local Chapter

Building the physician-scientist community at UC Irvine.

We are the UC Irvine local chapter of the American Physician Scientists Association — a student-run chapter that mentors, supports and advocates for medical students pursuing research careers. Open to every M.D. student at UCI, single-degree and dual-degree alike. No dues.

Our mission

Programming that is useful, and that did not exist before.

The mission of the local chapter is to mentor, support, and advocate for doctorate of medicine students who are physician-scientist aspirants at UC Irvine, with programming that is beneficial but not yet widely available or formalized. Article II, Chapter Constitution

Medical students interested in research often have to assemble the path themselves — finding a mentor, learning how a research career is actually funded, working out where a single-degree M.D. fits alongside a dual-degree track. The chapter exists to make that path visible and shared rather than something each student works out alone.

What we do

Four of the seven committees that run the chapter.

Every event the chapter holds is run by a committee and tied to a specific part of our mission.

Mentorship

Peer-to-peer and near-peer mentorship connecting members across every stage of training, so nobody has to work the path out alone.

Research

Chances to present your work, get real feedback from peers and faculty, and build toward the chapter's annual research symposium.

Career Planning

Advising sessions and panels on training pathways, specialty selection, funding, and the parts of a research career nobody puts in a brochure.

Advocacy

Science-policy education and organized participation in initiatives such as State Capitol Day, representing trainees to institutional and elected leadership.

Membership

If you are a UCI medical student interested in research, you qualify.

That is the whole test. You do not need a publication, a lab, or a dual-degree acceptance letter.

Open to single and dual degree
M.D., M.D./Ph.D., M.D./M.S., M.D./M.Eng. and M.D./M.P.H. students are all eligible and given equal consideration.
No membership dues
The chapter is a student interest group within the School of Medicine, so membership costs nothing.
Active membership
Attend 60% of events in an academic year to vote and stand for office. Exceptions are granted for clinical rotations and research commitments.
Elections each spring
Officers serve one-year terms and are elected by anonymous ballot. Any active member may be nominated, including themselves.

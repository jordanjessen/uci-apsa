Get Involved

Joining takes about two minutes.

There are no dues, no prerequisites and no application to be judged on.

Step one

Add your name to the roster

Fill in the membership form and tell us which committees interest you. You will be added to the mailing list and hear about every event first.

Eligibility

Who can join

Any UCI medical doctorate student
M.D., M.D./Ph.D., M.D./M.S., M.D./M.Eng. and M.D./M.P.H. students are all eligible, in good academic and conduct standing.
Single-degree students especially
You do not need to be in a dual-degree program to belong here. The constitution requires equal consideration of single-degree and dual-degree interests.
No dues, ever
The chapter is a student interest group within the School of Medicine, so membership is free.
Alumni and guests
Alumni are welcome to take part in chapter activities as invited guests, though they do not vote or hold office.

Step two: pick a committee

Committees are where the actual work happens, and they are the fastest way to meet people. Each is chaired by an officer and needs at least one general member to plan and run events. You can join more than one.

  • Mentorship — near-peer and peer-to-peer mentoring.
  • Research — the annual symposium and scholarly programming.
  • Technology — AI and large language models in research practice.
  • Professional Development — speaker series and workshops.
  • Career Planning — advising sessions and career panels.
  • Advocacy — science policy and State Capitol Day.
  • Fundraising — partnerships and funding for programming.

What each committee does

Becoming an active member

Attending 60% of the chapter's events in an academic year makes you an active member, which carries the right to vote, stand for office and serve on committees. The executive committee grants exceptions for clinical rotations, significant research commitments and approved leave — so a demanding year does not cost you your standing. The membership year runs from 1 July to 30 June.

Standing for office

Officer elections happen every spring, and self-nomination is explicitly allowed. If you have been a member for a year and have an idea about what the chapter should be doing, that is enough to put your name forward.

Joining national APSA as well

Local chapter membership is separate from national APSA membership. Joining nationally gets you the annual meeting, regional meetings and the wider network beyond UCI. We recommend both.

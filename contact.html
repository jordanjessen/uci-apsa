Who handles what

Everything reaches us through the same address — this is just what happens to your message next.

Membership and general questions
Handled by the Vice President, who maintains the membership roster and mailing list.
Event ideas and committee work
Handled by the Secretary, who oversees the committees that plan individual events.
Funding, sponsorship and budgets
Handled by the Treasurer, who manages chapter finances and AMSG funding requests.
School of Medicine matters
Handled by the School of Medicine Liaison, our link to the school and to single-degree students.
National APSA and institutional representation
Handled by the President, who serves as UC Irvine's Institutional Representative.
Speaking or collaborating with us
Faculty, other student groups and outside organizations are all welcome to write to the chapter address.

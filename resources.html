Funding, fellowships and awards

How research careers are actually paid for — the part of the path that is least discussed and hardest to work out alone.

  • NIH Loan Repayment Programs Repays qualifying student loan debt for researchers who commit to qualifying research. Currently capped by statute at $50,000 per year.
  • American Society for Clinical Investigation Honor society for physician-scientists, with awards, a journal and programming aimed at trainees.
  • Burroughs Wellcome Fund Private foundation funding career awards at the interface of basic biomedical science and clinical medicine.
  • Lasker Foundation Awards Background on the awards and, more usefully for trainees, on what a career in medical research can look like at its far end.
